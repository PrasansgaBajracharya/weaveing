Community Guidelines

A few simple principles for keeping weave.ing useful, respectful and creator-friendly.

Keep patterns original

Only upload work you have the right to share. Do not present another creator’s pattern as your own.

Respect other creators

Give creators credit where appropriate and keep discussions constructive. Harassment, spam and misleading content do not belong in the community.

Keep listings clear

Pattern titles, descriptions, images and prices should accurately represent the digital pattern being offered.
