About weave.ing

A simple digital space where crochet creators can share, discover, buy and sell digital crochet patterns.

Made for crochet creators

weave.ing brings digital crochet patterns into one community-focused marketplace. Creators can publish their work, manage their patterns and earnings, while buyers can discover patterns and keep purchased files in their library.

What we care about

  • Original and clearly presented crochet patterns.
  • A welcoming community for creators and learners.
  • Simple digital delivery for purchased patterns.
  • A marketplace experience designed around crochet.
