Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about buying, selling and using weave.ing.

What can I buy on weave.ing?

weave.ing is focused on digital crochet patterns. Purchased patterns are available through your library.

Can I sell my own crochet patterns?

Yes. Registered users can post digital crochet patterns. Submitted patterns may be reviewed before they appear for marketplace sale.

Are free patterns available?

Yes. A creator can publish a pattern as free or set a price for a paid pattern.

Where can I find patterns I purchased?

Your purchased digital patterns are available from your library after the purchase process is completed.

How do I get help?

Use the contact page to reach the weave.ing team.
